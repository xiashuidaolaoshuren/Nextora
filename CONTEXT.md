# Workspace Core

The workspace helps a candidate understand job openings, organize supporting evidence, and track application attempts without losing context.

## Language

### People and opportunities

**Candidate**:
The person whose job search, preferences, and evidence are represented in the workspace.
_Avoid_: Applicant when no application attempt is meant

**Job**:
A specific opening the candidate may consider or apply for. A Job can exist independently of an Application; identical descriptions do not necessarily identify the same opening.
_Avoid_: Application, job description

**Job description**:
The source text describing an opening and its conditions.
_Avoid_: Job when referring only to the text

**Poster**:
The employer or recruiter presenting a Job; the Poster is not necessarily the Hiring Company.
_Avoid_: Hiring Company when the employer is not known

**Hiring Company**:
The organization hiring for a Job, which may be different from the Poster.
_Avoid_: Poster when referring to the hiring organization

**Application**:
One attempt by a Candidate to apply for a Job. A Job may have multiple Applications, including a later reapplication.
_Avoid_: Saved Job, opening

### Candidate records

**Candidate Profile**:
The Candidate's self-reported facts and job preferences, including Work Permission, Graduation Dates, Full-time Work Experience, and language abilities where known.
_Avoid_: CV, Evidence Library

**Work Permission**:
A Candidate's self-reported current permission to work in a jurisdiction, including whether it is restricted. It is distinct from residency, visa status, and whether a new employer needs to apply for approval.
_Avoid_: Visa status as a synonym for permission

**Sponsorship to Start**:
Whether a new employer would need to apply for a work visa or approval before the Candidate could start work. It says nothing about employer support for a future extension.
_Avoid_: Unqualified sponsorship need

**Graduation Date**:
The Candidate's self-reported date of graduation for a particular degree, marked as completed or expected.
_Avoid_: Career stage

**Full-time Work Experience**:
The Candidate's self-reported total duration of full-time work, across roles. It is distinct from experience in a particular role, field, or skill.

**Evidence Item**:
A Candidate claim about experience, education, skills, projects, or credentials. Evidence belongs to the Candidate rather than to a particular CV, although a CV may be its source.
_Avoid_: CV version, job requirement

**Confirmed Evidence**:
An Evidence Item the Candidate has explicitly approved for use in matching.
_Avoid_: Suggested evidence

**Suggested Evidence**:
An Evidence Item proposed for Candidate review that has not yet been confirmed.
_Avoid_: Confirmed Evidence

**Evidence Library**:
The Candidate's collection of Evidence Items and their review states.
_Avoid_: CV

**CV Version**:
A specific version of a Candidate's CV. The version submitted with an Application can be recorded independently of the Candidate's current default CV.
_Avoid_: Evidence Item, Evidence Library

### Assessment

**Eligibility**:
An advisory assessment of whether self-reported Candidate facts satisfy explicit conditions stated for a Job; unknown facts or conditions do not establish a failure.
_Avoid_: Preference Match, legal determination

**Preference Match**:
A comparison of a Job's characteristics with the Candidate's stated preferences, separate from Eligibility.
_Avoid_: Eligibility

**Job Requirement**:
A condition or qualification expressed for a Job, classified where possible as required or nice to have.
_Avoid_: Evidence Item

**Evidence Fit**:
A requirement-by-requirement assessment of how the Candidate's confirmed evidence supports a Job. It assesses the Candidate, not whether a selected CV presents that evidence.
_Avoid_: CV coverage, fit score
