# Keep candidate evidence independent of CV versions

Confirmed Evidence belongs to the Candidate, not to a particular CV Version. This lets the same approved claim support multiple Jobs and Applications and survive removal of a source CV, rather than requiring separate confirmation for each document. CV imports propose Evidence for review and cannot silently change confirmed claims; Evidence Fit assesses the Candidate's evidence rather than the contents of a selected CV.

A CV Version remains a separate document that can be pinned to an Application. Archiving is its default removal action; deliberate permanent deletion removes its content while retaining a minimal marker for Application history.
